# Code of conduct

This project has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in the project - in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space - is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make participation a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **admin@ankra.io**. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly,
and the privacy and security of the reporter are respected. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that
do not follow this code of conduct and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they deem
harmful.
